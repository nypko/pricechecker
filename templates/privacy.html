Privacy

Short version: DoorPrice sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and has no accounts, so it collects no personal data about you.

What is stored on your device

Your choices for currency, ship-to country and payment method are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them next time. They never leave your device and you can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

On your first visit the ship-to country and currency are picked from your device's time zone. This is worked out in your browser; we don't look up your IP address or location.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Fonts and all other files are served from DoorPrice itself, not from third-party services.

When you click through to a store

Store links open the store's own website. Some are affiliate links, so the store or its affiliate network may set cookies to recognise that you came from DoorPrice. From that point the store's own privacy policy applies. Nothing about you is passed from DoorPrice to the store beyond the link you clicked.
