About DoorPrice

DoorPrice shows what a flagship phone from an import store really costs once it reaches your door: the store's price plus payment fees, shipping and estimated import tax for your country.

Who runs it

DoorPrice is an independent project based in {{ site.country }}. It isn't owned by, or part of, any store or phone brand.

We track {{ phones|length }} current flagship phones at {{ connected|length }} specialist import stores: {% for s in connected %}{{ s.label }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. Many of these phones come in a Global and a China version, so we show both side by side at the same RAM and storage.

How prices are checked

  • Every hour an automated checker reads each store's public product data. It identifies itself to stores, follows their robots.txt rules and keeps a slow, polite pace.
  • Store terms (payment fees and discounts, shipping costs, who pays import tax) come from each store's own payment, shipping and customs pages and are re-checked daily. Where buyers' experience differs from what a store promises, the page says so.
  • Currencies: we use a store's own price in your currency where it sets one; other prices are converted at European Central Bank rates and marked ≈.

How the delivered total is worked out

For your chosen country and payment method: the store's price, plus or minus its payment fee or discount, plus shipping, plus estimated import VAT or GST when the store's price doesn't include it. When something isn't known, such as a store that doesn't publish its shipping cost, the total says so ("+ shipping", "tax?") instead of guessing. Always confirm the final price at the store before you pay. Every step, with the tax rate for each country.

How DoorPrice earns money

Some links to stores are affiliate links: if you buy after clicking one, the store may pay us a commission. It costs you nothing extra. Commissions never change the order: stores are always ranked by the total you would pay, and stores without an affiliate deal are listed the same way. We don't accept payment for placement and don't publish sponsored reviews.
