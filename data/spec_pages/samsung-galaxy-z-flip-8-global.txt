Frequently Asked Questions
Got questions? Here is what you're looking for
What are the key features of Galaxy Z Flip8?
Galaxy Z Flip8 delivers a lightweight, foldable experience.
• It is our lightest and thinnest Flip ever at 180 grams and 6.1 millimeters. The lightweight design means you can carry it comfortably in your hand or pocket throughout the day.
• The advanced camera system delivers a DSLR-like shooting experience thanks to an AI-powered bokeh effect, shake-free videos with Super Steady and quick and easy editing with Galaxy AI features like Photo Assist.
• The powerful Exynos 2600 processor is customized especially for Galaxy with improved NPU, GPU and CPU for smoother graphics.
17
What is the difference between Galaxy Z Flip7 and Galaxy Z Flip8?
If you value a slim, foldable design, the Galaxy Z Flip8 is a compelling upgrade choice.
• It is 8 grams lighter than Galaxy Z Flip7, 6.1 mm thick when unfolded and easier to open, making it more pocketable.
• It features an elevated camera system thanks to tools like Photo Assist, Super Steady and Flip Shot.
• FlexWindow is optimized for One UI 9, allowing for more customizations and layouts that seamlessly match your home screen.
• The Armor FlexHinge received a reduction in thickness, delivering a reduced friction mechanism, which preserves the overall hinge design.
18
What colors does Galaxy Z Flip8 come in?
Galaxy Z Flip8 comes in a set of premium color options for you to choose from, depending on your taste.
• These include Pink, Graphite and Cream.
19
How durable is Galaxy Z Flip8?
Galaxy Z Flip8 is fortified with materials that strengthen the device for long-lasting durability.
• The Armor Aluminum frame provides enhanced protection for the device’s internal components and exterior edges.
• Engineered using an ion-strengthened glass formula, Corning® Gorilla® Glass Victus® 2 has been applied to the front of the device for impact protection without compromising visual clarity.
• The Armor FlexHinge is designed to be thin but very rigid.
• The IP48 rating helps provide water resistance.
20
Can I customize Galaxy Z Flip8's cover screen?
Yes, you can customize Galaxy Z Flip8’s cover screen to match your style and do more without having to unfold the device.
• Customize the cover screen with more widgets on a cover screen that now seamlessly matches your home screen layout.
• You can also view your personalized insights directly on FlexWindow. The Now Brief card displays a summary of what matters most to you. This gives you a quick glance at key information without having to open other apps, helping you stay organized and focused throughout the day.
21
What is the best case for Galaxy Z Flip8?
Galaxy Z Flip8 works best with official Samsung accessories, such as Flipsuit Cases and Magnet Cases, for convenience and everyday protection.
• Flipsuit Cases come with interactive Flipsuit Cards that automatically change FlexWindow’s background to match the design on the card.
• Magnet Cases have a built-in magnetic ring that helps your phone align perfectly with compatible wireless chargers and magnetic accessories.
• This makes charging easier, more reliable and allows you to attach accessories securely.
How many megapixels is Galaxy Z Flip8?
Galaxy Z Flip8 has three different camera sensors that each play an important role depending on your needs.
• 50 MP Wide is perfect for high-resolution photos and advanced detail.
• 12 MP Ultra Wide is good for capturing expansive landscapes and group photos.
• The 10 MP main screen camera delivers clear, detailed selfies and video calls.
22
Is Galaxy Z Flip8 good for taking videos at night?
Yes, Galaxy Z Flip8 is equipped with a powerful camera system that combines improved hardware with intelligent software, making it good for taking videos at night.
• The ProVisual Engine automatically enhances Nightography video quality, so no special settings are required.
• It applies noise reduction, stability and image processing to further enhance the color and details in videos that are shot in low light.
23
What is Flip Shot and how does it work?
Flip Shot is a customization feature that allows you to display photos, animations or text on the cover screen while taking pictures.
• Instead of the standard camera preview on the cover screen, Flip Shot lets you personalize FlexWindow after being downloaded and enabled in the camera settings.
• Customize the cover screen with an image, GIF or text that can be seen while taking mirror selfies, allowing you to add more of your personality to the photo.
What AI features are available on Galaxy Z Flip8?
Galaxy Z Flip8 comes with updated Galaxy AI features that are easy and simple to use.
• You can enjoy fast photo editing with your own personalized prompts in Photo Assist. Simply describe the edit you want to make, and Galaxy AI will enhance it.
• Now Brief now comes with buttons on certain insights that suggest actions such as creating a card for a friend's upcoming birthday.
24
What languages does Photo Assist support?
As of July 2026, Galaxy AI's Photo Assist supports 41 languages which are listed below.
• Photo Assist supported languages: Korean, Arabic, Bengali, Bulgarian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Latvian, Lithuanian, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Serbian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese, Filipino and Gujarati.
What languages does Now Brief support?
As of July 2026, Galaxy AI's Now Brief supports 13 languages which are listed below.
• Now Brief supported languages: Korean, English, Spanish, Italian, German, French, Portuguese, Polish, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Thai, Hindi and Vietnamese.
How long does the battery last on Galaxy Z Flip8?
Galaxy Z Flip8’s battery is optimized to provide a long battery life for lasting use.
• A typical battery capacity of 4300 mAh allows you to comfortably work, play and browse throughout the day without needing to recharge.
• With substantial video playback endurance, you can watch your favorites for up to 31 hours.
25
What storage options are available for Galaxy Z Flip8?
Galaxy Z Flip8 has two storage options available: 512 GB and 256 GB.
• 512 GB comes with 12 GB of memory.
• 256 GB comes with 12 GB of memory.
26
Who is Galaxy Z Flip8 best suited for?
Galaxy Z Flip8 is best suited for users that like to express their personality and want ultimate pocketability.
• The compact, thin yet stylish design slips easily into your pocket or bag.
• Regular vloggers can use camera features such as Dual Recording and Super Steady to elevate their footage.
• Everyday photographers can take natural, hands-free selfies with friends without having to worry about bringing a tripod or setting any timers.
• The cover screen is highly customizable, allowing you to tailor wallpaper, widgets and color schemes that suit your personality.
How does Galaxy Z Flip8 help me easily take selfies with friends?
Galaxy Z Flip8's design allows you to effortlessly take selfies or group shots with friends.
•FlexMode is the built-in tripod mode that lets you to take photos with the rear camera hands-free and see a preview on the cover screen.
•You can also use the palm gesture while in FlexMode to take photos at a distance with hand movements without the need for timers.
•Auto Zoom uses AI to detect subjects and zoom either in or out to help achieve better framing for your group shots.
•Flip Shot elevates your mirror selfies by allowing you to customize the cover screen when taking photos.
27
How can I express my style and personality with Galaxy Z Flip8?
There are a few ways you can express yourself and show off your personality with Galaxy Z Flip8.
• Optimized for One UI 9, the home screen layout also now seamlessly integrates with the cover screen so widget customizations are available from FlexWindow, making it easier to personalize your Flip.
• Plus, Now Brief is available from FlexWindow for AI-powered personalization that allows you to do many small tasks while keeping the phone folded.
28
How does Galaxy Z Flip8 help me effortlessly create content?
Galaxy Z Flip8 has video features that help you effortlessly capture the moment from different perspectives.
• Use Super Steady’s Horizontal Lock feature while filming a day-in-the-life style vlog. It is designed to keep the camera level and stable during handheld shooting so you can capture smooth footage while moving.
• Dual Recording lets you record two videos at once. Originally offered in a top-to-bottom split screen, you can now also record in a left-to-right split view. This lets you see both sides without switching screens, giving you a different way to capture moments.
29
I like to capture the moment. How versatile is Galaxy Z Flip8's camera?
Galaxy Z Flip8’s unique form factor allows for a variety of filming styles when making videos.
• Holding your Flip horizontally in a camcorder grip style gives you quick access to the record buttons and easy zoom control with an on-display zoom slider.
• Dual Recording allows both you and your subjects to see both perspectives for fun, creative videos that are ideal to share with friends on social media.
• FlexCam lets you go hands-free with a built-in tripod mode while you and your subjects get in the frame.
I like to get creative with pictures. Does Galaxy Z Flip8 have AI photo editing?
Yes, you can use Photo Assist on Galaxy Z Flip8, which is boosted by AI.
• In Photo Assist, you can move, resize or remove objects or fill in backgrounds for effortless photo touch-ups.
• You can now also use written prompts in the Create section for personalized editing.
30
I'm switching to Galaxy for the first time. How easy is it?
You can effortlessly transfer your data to Galaxy Z Flip8, even from smartphones with a different OS such as iOS.
• Contacts, photos, music, videos, messages, notes and calendars can all be easily transferred.
• When you are ready to switch phones, use the upgraded switching experience to transfer your data wirelessly. Transfer more data types than before, including photos, videos, contacts, call history and home screen layouts. Just scan the QR code that comes up during setup.
• The simple process means you can start using your new device with your previous data right away.
