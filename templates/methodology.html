How prices are worked out

Every total on DoorPrice is the price you would pay to get the phone to your door: the store's price, the fee or discount for how you pay, shipping, and the import tax due on delivery. This page shows exactly how each part is calculated and where the numbers come from.

The delivered total

  1. Store price for the RAM/storage and version you picked, in your currency where the store sets one; otherwise converted at the European Central Bank rate and marked ≈. Store-wide automatic discounts are applied.
  2. Payment fee or discount from the store's own payment page (for example a bank-transfer discount or a PayPal surcharge). A method the store doesn't accept for that order is shown as not available.
  3. Shipping to your Ship-to destination from the store's shipping page. If a store only says "shipping from €10", the total says so.
  4. Import tax, when the store's price doesn't include it: the VAT or GST rate of your destination applied to the phone plus shipping, the way customs values an import, plus the courier's clearance fee where the courier publishes one. Estimates are marked ≈.

When we can't know a part, the total says so instead of guessing: "+ shipping" means the store doesn't publish its shipping cost, and "tax?" means the store doesn't say whether import tax is included.

Import tax by destination

Phones (customs code 8517.13) have 0% customs duty in the EU, the UK, Canada and Australia, so only VAT, GST or HST is due. A phone costs more than every low-value limit (€150 in the EU, £135 in the UK, C$20 in Canada, A$1,000 in Australia), so the tax is always charged on import, normally collected by the courier before delivery.

When a store says import tax is included

Some stores pay the import tax themselves (delivered duty paid) and say so on their customs page; those totals have no tax added. A shipping line advertised as "tax-free" is not the same thing: it can mean the parcel is declared below its value, which leaves the buyer liable if customs checks it. We only treat tax as included when the store's own terms say it pays it.

How stores are ranked

  1. Offers you can pay for with your chosen method come first.
  2. Then totals that are complete (shipping and import tax known) come ahead of ones that leave something open, so a lower-looking price with unknown shipping doesn't hide the real cheapest. The cheaper open offer is still shown ("or €X + shipping at …").
  3. Then the lowest total wins.

Affiliate commissions never change the order, and stores without an affiliate deal are ranked the same way.

How a drop is counted

The Price drops page compares each store listing with itself: the same store, the same version (Global or China) and the same RAM and storage. A listing counts as a drop when its price is at least {{ drop_pct }}% and €{{ drop_eur }} lower than it was 7 days before, both at once, so a small move on a cheap phone or a fraction of a percent on an expensive one is left out. Prices are compared in euros at the European Central Bank rate. A store we only started tracking in the last 7 days has no earlier price to compare with, so it is never a drop. These are store prices, before shipping, payment fees and import tax.

The Telegram channel works from the delivered price instead. {% if site.telegram %}DoorPrice on Telegram{% else %}The Telegram channel{% endif %} posts a phone when its cheapest delivered price to the EU, paid by card, falls at least {{ tg_pct }}% and €{{ tg_eur }} below the price at the last post about that phone. Smaller moves add up: the comparison stays at the last post until the price has fallen far enough.

Global, China and Asia versions

A Global version is made for sale worldwide, with Google apps and broad network bands. A China version runs the brand's Chinese software; on most brands Google Play can be switched on in Settings, and the phone page says how for its brand. For Samsung and Sony we say Asia version, because one model number covers China, Hong Kong and Taiwan and the store doesn't say which software it ships. The maker's warranty is usually tied to the region the phone was sold in.

Specs

Chip, screen, battery and charging are entered by hand from the brand's spec page or from launch coverage where at least two sources agree (such as GSMArena and Notebookcheck). A field the sources disagree on is left out rather than guessed. Where a version or market has a different battery, each is shown on its own line.

Which stores we list

A store is added when it sells current flagships to buyers abroad, publishes its shipping, payment and import-tax terms, has a track record buyers can check, and allows its public product data to be read: our checker follows each store's robots.txt and never gets around bot protection. Prices are checked every hour; store terms are re-checked regularly and dated.
