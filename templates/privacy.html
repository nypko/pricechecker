Privacy

Short version: DoorPrice sets no cookies, runs no advertising trackers and has no accounts, so it collects no personal data about you. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or identify you.

What is stored on your device

Your choices for currency, ship-to country and payment method are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them next time. They never leave your device and you can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Phones you choose to Watch are saved the same way, in your browser, with the price you last saw so the list can show how it has moved. Nothing is sent to us; clearing this site's data removes the watchlist. Price change feeds (RSS) are plain files: your feed reader fetches them like any other page.

On your first visit the ship-to country and currency are picked from your device's time zone. This is worked out in your browser; we don't look up your IP address or location.

Delivery reports you send us

If you use “How did your order arrive?” on a phone page, we store only what the form asks: the phone, store, destination country, days to arrive, shipping method and any import tax you paid, with the date. We don't store your name, email or IP address. We check each report before it counts, and publish only totals and ranges, not single reports.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Fonts and all other files are served from DoorPrice itself, not from third-party services.

Visit statistics

To see which pages are read and which links bring people here, Cloudflare adds its Web Analytics script to every page. It counts page views, the referring site, browser and country, and page-load speed. It sets no cookies, doesn't use local storage, doesn't fingerprint your device and doesn't follow you to other sites; we only see totals, never a single visitor. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

When you click through to a store

Store links open the store's own website. Some are affiliate links, so the store or its affiliate network may set cookies to recognise that you came from DoorPrice. From that point the store's own privacy policy applies. Nothing about you is passed from DoorPrice to the store beyond the link you clicked.
