Warranty
Global International warranty from many sellers. Check the store.
China Usually no manufacturer warranty outside China. You rely on the store's own warranty and returns.
Networks & SIM
Global Built for networks worldwide; eSIM where the model has it.
China May miss some 4G/5G bands your carrier uses, and often has no eSIM. Check the band list before you buy.
Language
China Can be set to English; some Chinese apps come preinstalled.

What's in the total?
Price
The store's own price in your currency. ≈ means the store has no price in that currency, so we converted it at the European Central Bank rate.
Payment
The store's fee or discount for how you pay, e.g. a discount for bank transfer or a PayPal fee.
Shipping
Shipping to your country, where the store lists it. If it doesn't, the total says “excl. shipping”.
Import tax
Some stores include import tax in their price; others leave it for you to pay the courier on delivery. Where the store excludes it, we add an estimate (≈).
