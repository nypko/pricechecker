EU band check
For each version we compare the 4G and 5G bands the brand lists on its own spec page with the main bands European networks use, and name any the phone lacks and why that band matters (for example 800 MHz for indoor and rural coverage). A band the brand's page doesn't list counts as missing. It is a guide, not a promise: operators differ, so check yours. The bands and the list we compare against are on the phone's specs page.
